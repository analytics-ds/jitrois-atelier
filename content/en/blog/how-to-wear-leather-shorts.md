Leather shorts intimidate more than they should. Well cut and well accompanied, they hold a place few pieces occupy, that of a short garment which stays structured. Here is **how to wear leather shorts** without ever feeling that you are forcing your look.

## Start with the cut and the length

Everything is decided here, before any question of tops or shoes.

A mid-thigh length is the easiest to live with, it keeps a clean line of leg and carries from morning to evening. A more generous length, closer to a bermuda, creates a very current tailored look and pairs perfectly with a structured jacket. Very short models exist too, they simply call for more coverage elsewhere, a long sleeve, a high neck.

The rise matters just as much. A high waist lengthens the silhouette and lets you tuck the top in, which builds the line immediately. A low waist calls for a longer top and a more relaxed silhouette.

## The top that balances the piece

The rule is easy to remember, the top should be softer than the shorts. Leather already brings material and light, so whatever sits above it should calm things down.

A supple knit, a fine roll neck, a fluid shirt worn tucked in, a tee in a fine quality, all of them work. Volume is then set as a mirror of the cut. Wide shorts go with a closer-fitting top, fitted shorts carry a more generous knit. It is the same principle of balance that governs the way you wear a [leather skirt](/en/blog/how-to-wear-a-leather-skirt/).

## The leg, where it is all decided

Shorts are judged as much by what surrounds them as by themselves, and the leg does half the work.

- The high boot extends the line and remains the safest choice
- Ankle boots call for care in the space between the hem and the shaft, an opaque tight resolves it
- A matte tight in a shade close to the shorts creates a continuity that makes the outfit dressier
- In warmer weather, a flat sandal with a fine strap or an understated mule leaves the leg bare without clutter

One remark from the fitting room, which we often repeat. The more worked the shoe, the simpler the shorts should be, and the reverse holds too.

## Leather shorts in winter

This is probably their best season, contrary to what one might imagine. Leather pairs naturally with thick knitwear, wool and warm materials, and the contrast between a covered leg and a short piece creates a very constructed silhouette.

The formula that almost always works combines high-waisted shorts, an opaque tight, a high boot and a generous jumper. A long coat on top brings the verticality that short outfits sometimes lack. You will recognise here the principles that govern an [all-leather look](/en/blog/how-to-wear-an-all-leather-look-women/), in a more measured version.

## Three occasions, three readings

For daytime, mid-thigh shorts, a supple knit, a flat ankle boot. The look is clean, almost self-evident.

For a dressier register, leather bermudas worn with a matching jacket make a short suit that replaces the classic tailored one to advantage.

For the evening, shorts become the contemporary alternative to the short skirt. A fluid top, an understated court shoe, a single piece of jewellery, nothing else. As with [leather leggings](/en/blog/how-to-wear-leather-leggings/), success rests on the restraint of everything around them.
